# Stay Research Workflow

## 1. Build the stay envelope

Record exact dates, nights, party and ages, room/bed/bathroom needs, accessibility, maximum budget, cancellation requirement, arrival and departure points and local times, luggage, and priority experiences.

Compute a preliminary usable window from realistic arrival clearance through the departure reporting buffer. Check-in, check-out, and luggage storage can change that window.

## 2. Cluster places before properties

Map priority places into geographic and time-of-day clusters. Distinguish:

- repeated anchors visited most days;
- fixed-time activities;
- one-off excursions;
- evening locations whose last return matters.

Create candidate neighborhoods that plausibly serve those clusters. A remote property may remain if one concentrated excursion replaces repeated commuting.

## 3. Verify exact bookable rates

For every retained property capture:

- property, address or map location, exact room and occupancy;
- dates, nights, meal inclusion, bed and bathroom;
- complete price and whether it is for the room or each guest;
- mandatory taxes, resort, cleaning, service, card, and destination fees;
- payment timing, deposit, cancellation deadline, and refundability;
- check-in/out, late-arrival procedure, and luggage storage;
- exact reproducible booking URL and research timestamp.

Use the checkout-equivalent total where safely visible. A cached snippet or property-level “from” price discovers candidates but does not substantiate a row. Confirm ambiguous totals with a second source.

## 4. Verify door-to-door mobility

Route each property at realistic travel times:

1. arrival point to property;
2. property to every repeated anchor;
3. fixed-time activities in both directions;
4. property to departure point with the required buffer.

Include walks, waits, transfers, service frequency, seasonal disruption, accessibility, and last return. Do not use straight-line distance as travel time.

## 5. Price the local-ticket strategy

From official operator sources capture:

- single fare and transfer rules;
- day or multi-day pass price and validity clock;
- required zones;
- airport/station inclusion or supplement;
- visitor-card transport and attraction inclusions;
- child/group rules;
- night-service or reservation charges;
- purchase method and current validity dates.

Calculate both plausible strategies:

`individual-ticket total = required journeys × applicable fares and supplements`

`pass total = passes for the party + excluded journeys and supplements`

Recommend the cheaper valid strategy. If trip counts or coverage are unknown, show the break-even point and label the total unavailable.

## 6. Normalize cost and time

`effective stay cost = accommodation + mandatory property taxes/fees + required arrival/departure transfers + routine local transport`

Keep optional meals, upgrades, parking, shopping, and activities outside this total unless required by the request.

`usable destination minutes = departure reporting cutoff - arrival clearance - unavoidable check-in/out or luggage gaps`

`routine local-travel minutes = transfer, wait, walk, and ride minutes required by the planned repeated routes`

`lost-time ratio = routine local-travel minutes / usable destination minutes`

Report the ratio as a percentage and show the underlying minutes. Do not monetize it.

## 7. Review quality and practical risk

Use recent review distributions and recurring themes. Record sample size, recency, and repeated issues relevant to sleep, cleanliness, safety, heating/cooling, noise, accessibility, or host reliability. A high score with few reviews has lower confidence than a slightly lower score with substantial recent evidence.

## 8. Rank the Pareto choices

Reject hard-constraint violations. Identify:

- cheapest acceptable property;
- fastest-location property by cumulative routine travel;
- price-first best-value property.

Quantify the recommended premium or saving, local-travel hours gained or lost, lost-time-ratio difference, and material terms or risks. Trip length changes the consequence of travel time; no universal distance cutoff replaces this calculation.

## 9. Verify and stop honestly

Open the deepest safe URLs that preserve the property, dates, party, room, and rate when possible. Link official transport evidence separately. If live availability, total price, routing, or pass scope cannot be verified, mark it unavailable and avoid calling the row bookable or cheapest.
