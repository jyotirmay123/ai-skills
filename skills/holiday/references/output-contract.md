# Holiday Comparison Output Contract

## Answer order

1. One-sentence recommendation with destination, dates, intercity option, exact property/rate, and complete EUR whole-party total.
2. Assumptions, allowance bases, and research timestamp.
3. Ranked coherent-package table.
4. Numeric cheapest, recommended, and lowest-travel comparison.
5. Day-level feasibility outline.
6. Booking order and checkout checks.

## Required package table

Use one row per coherent combination, not one row per independent component.

| Rank | Dates and package | Intercity transport | Exact stay | Transfers and local transport | Activities and food | Time and pace | Complete total | Verify |
|---:|---|---|---|---|---|---|---:|---|

Each row contains:

- transparent whole-party EUR arithmetic;
- per-person total and cost per usable destination day;
- local arrival/departure times and relevant date offsets;
- accommodation room/rate, nights, mandatory fees, and cancellation;
- airport/station transfer and local ticket/pass calculation;
- selected fixed activities and food-allowance basis;
- intercity time, usable destination time, routine local-travel time, longest routine one-way journey, and lost-time ratio;
- pace assessment and material risks;
- exact transport/property links plus official transit/activity evidence.

Mark estimates with `≈` and unresolved required costs as `unavailable`. When a required component is unavailable, label the figure a subtotal and do not rank it as the proven cheapest complete package.

## Decision summary

State numerically:

- cheapest acceptable complete whole-party total;
- recommended total and premium or saving;
- usable hours and routine local-travel hours gained or lost;
- lost-time-ratio difference;
- lowest-travel/most-relaxing total when distinct.

If one package wins every dimension, say so plainly.

## Day-level feasibility

Provide a light outline, not a minute-by-minute schedule. For each destination day show the compatible activity cluster, necessary transfer, meaningful free/rest time, and fixed closing or last-return constraint. The outline demonstrates feasibility and pace; it does not add unrequested activities.

## Compact example

“Choose Flight A plus Central Hotel for €816 total for two. It costs €14 more than the absolute cheapest coherent package but restores 2.25 usable hours, cuts routine travel from 3 hours to 45 minutes, and lowers the lost-time ratio from 6.7% to 1.6%.”

The figures illustrate the format only; never reuse them as live data.

## Booking order

List the recommended purchase sequence and explain the controlling dependency, such as limited flight inventory, non-refundable lodging, timed activity availability, or transfer service.

## Checkout checks

Tell the user to confirm:

1. Every displayed price still applies to the same dates, party, room/rate, itinerary, baggage, and terms.
2. The EUR total includes mandatory taxes, fees, transfers, local transport, selected activities, and the stated food allowance.
3. Arrival, check-in, last transport, checkout, luggage, and departure times remain compatible.
4. Transit zones, pass validity, airport coverage, and activity opening times match the outline.
5. Protected tickets and refundable components remain as described.
6. Final payment pages have not changed any component or introduced fees.
