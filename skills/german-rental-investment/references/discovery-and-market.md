# Listing discovery, locality and valuation protocol

## Search scope and source hierarchy

Search nationally unless the user restricts cities. Use current owner/agent listings on portals (e.g., ImmoScout24, Immowelt, Kleinanzeigen, local brokers, bank and court auction listings where relevant). Respect sites' terms; use browser/search access normally available to the host, **not unauthorized scraping or invented API access**. Capture the primary source URL, observed timestamp and whether it is still advertised. De-duplicate by address/approximate coordinates, size, images and agent reference; don't confuse a relisted asking price with a transaction.

Match the unit to an investable residential letting strategy: whole flat, WG, furnished, mid-term, student housing, or operator lease. The legal regime is strategy-specific and cannot be inferred from the marketing label. For any existing tenant, establish current paid Kaltmiete, tenancy start, tenant protections and whether the investment is delivered vacant. Reject "market-rent potential" as current contractual rent.

## Normalize candidate evidence

Gather or label unknown:
- Street/quarter (mask exact address in public report), municipality, Bundesland, listing URL/date and physical inspection opportunity.
- Kaufpreis, acquisition costs, broker fee, renovation/furnishing, Wohnfläche, purchase €/m², year built, renovated year, floor, lift, accessibility, balcony, parking, tenure, Erbbaurecht status.
- Leases, current Kaltmiete, separately collectible parking rent, actual Betriebskosten/Hausgeld reconciliation, non-umlagefähige Kosten, management and reserves.
- Energy class, heating and possible mandatory upgrades; structural problems; damp/flood, insurance exclusions, special assessments.
- WEG Teilungserklärung, latest Wirtschaftspläne/Jahresabrechnungen, 3–5 years' Eigentümerversammlung minutes, maintenance reserve, arrears and planned works.
- Existing occupancy and lease-level supporting evidence, any subsidized/price-controlled housing, vacancy history, utility split.
- Guarantee/insurance instrument and counterparty documents, cancellation and renewal terms, claims exclusions, security and insolvency protection.

Quality labels: **A** primary dated government record or executed document; **B** independent recent sourced market evidence; **C** advertiser statement; **D** assumption. Do not grade a listing BUY if any decisive assumption is C/D.

## Price and local growth research

1. Use the local **Gutachterausschuss**, Immobilienmarktbericht, property transaction indicators and BORIS/official Bodenrichtwerte where available. Land value is not apartment sale price; published averages are not a substitute for unit-level comparables.
2. Find at least 3–5 defensible same-quarter or directly comparable unit observations. Distinguish **closed transactions** from **asking listings**, disclose sample count and observation period; adjust for floor, year, energy, lift, fit-out, parking, occupancy, legal rent and WEG status.
3. Derive independent fair-value **range**, conservative current resale price and a maximum offer using downside cash-flow/return requirements. Include sales friction; do not take a broker's "bank valuation" as independent evidence.
4. Compare 5–10-year population/household formation and migration, median incomes, university and employment demand, diversification of employers, wage-paying capacity, housing completions/pipeline, measured vacancy, transit and committed public investment. Distinguish correlation from causal proof of price growth.
5. Penalize shrinking areas with high gross yields but weak tenant turnover, employer concentration, deferred maintenance and resale illiquidity. Reward resilience only when verified by evidence.
6. Climate/environmental checks: flood map, heavy-rain surface water, contamination/radon as relevant, heat stress, energy renovation liability, building insurance and expected capex.
7. Mark marketing forecasts, "regeneration zones", headline infrastructure announcements and short-time price series as uncertain. Show -1%, 0%, +2% nominal annual appreciation as **illustrative scenarios**, then replace with evidence-based ranges if better local data are available.

## Funnel and ranking

Longlist roughly 10–20 reasonably varied active listings where availability allows. Pre-filter before detailed calculations: asking price and full costs, verified legal rent, realistic debt, capex and liquid reserves, existing portfolio concentration, and seller quality. Carry 3–5 finalists to full underwriting; if fewer survive, explain the rejected count rather than inventing choices.

Never interpret a portal's reported "rental yield" or regional average €/m² as legal achievable Kaltmiete for this exact unit. Retain source links for every candidate and note the date of verification.
