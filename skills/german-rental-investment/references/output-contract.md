# Required evidence and decision report

Start with one unambiguous sentence: **BUY [address masked] / NEGOTIATE to <= €X / WATCH / REJECT / INSUFFICIENT DATA / BUY NOTHING**, and the **binding reason**. A "BUY" requires independently verified decision-critical evidence, not merely a computed score.

## Comparison table

For each candidate present:
- Listing link, date checked, city/neighbourhood, unit type, sqm, asking and full all-in price, €/m².
- Independent sale-comparable range, net cold rent today, max legally supportable rent €/m² and month, law/ordinance/Mietspiegel URL and checked date; any disputed WG/furnished status.
- Current lease, rent guarantee source/provider/security/expiry, true post-guarantee rent.
- HOA/Hausgeld recovered and nonrecovered, owner reserve/capex and suspected Sonderumlage.
- Debt structure with nominal/effective APR, split payments, fixation and balance at year 10; closing funds available.
- First-year pretax cash flow per month, after-tax estimate **only if verified**, DSCR, at-zero-price-growth exit IRR with correct tax caveats, downside liquidity draw.
- Data confidence, red flags, decision and **maximum defensible offer**, along with the exact condition to upgrade.

## Detail for each finalist

1. **Source ledger** — every material data point, URL/document, date, and A/B/C/D confidence. Never pass an advertised number off as independently verified.
2. **Legality** — ordinance, local rent table, exact flat attributes, permitted Kaltmiete, evidence for exceptions, tenant contracts, room/furnished breakdown, proposed increases.
3. **Property/WEG** — building condition, energy and future law, owner costs, plans and reserve, community disputes/arrears, financing documents, notarial and land registry matters.
4. **Financing and taxes** — price-to-value and all-in basis; each loan's actual repayment; signed/unsigned distinction; sound land/building allocation, proven AfA, incremental household tax **relative to current portfolio** and no-tax-benefit scenario. Show monthly cash both before and after taxes.
5. **10-year scenario table** — property and combined portfolio rent cashflows, outstanding debt, principal paydown, reserve lowest point, annual salary stress, rate shocks, exit cost/tax under downside / 0%-appreciation / upside. Document all rent rises and law-driven limits; omit any unsupported probability.
6. **Verdict** — why this improves expected household financial resilience vs no new purchase, absolute deal breakers, lender/legal/Steuerberater questions, and a precise bid ceiling.

Always show liquidity and all annual totals in EUR, disclose uncertainty and distinguish cash flow from paper tax benefit and capital appreciation. If 0 listings clear the gates, return **BUY NOTHING**, with a watchlist and evidence-based price/rent targets. Do not invent a score, confidence percentage, list price, legal conclusion, guaranteed yield or future offer availability.
