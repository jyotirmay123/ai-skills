# German rental law, AfA, taxes and rent guarantees

**Law is dynamic.** At every run verify the acquisition year, Bundesland/municipal ordinance validity, local Mietspiegel, property dates and current tax law. This document is a research checklist, not a claim that a rule applies to every apartment. Prefer statutory sources and dated official city/state publications.

## Legally collectible rent: two distinct gates

**New lease rent / Mietpreisbremse.** BGB § 556d generally limits starting residential rent to the local comparative rent +10% **only** in areas designated by a currently effective Land ordinance. Locate the current ordinance and applicability dates for the *specific address*. § 556e can preserve qualifying prior rent or qualifying modernisation increases; § 556f treats certain first-used post-1 Oct 2014 housing and the first letting after comprehensive modernisation differently. § 556g imposes disclosure requirements for exceptions. Verify documentary evidence and avoid inventing an exception.

**In-tenancy rent increases.** BGB § 558 has its own conditions and comparative-rent ceiling, generally a 20% three-year cap or 15% where a valid tighter ordinance applies. Do not assume a 10% or 15% increase every 3 years, automatic rent compounding, or unlimited increase after a guarantee expires. Distinguish Staffel-/Indexmiete, energy/modernisation increases and subsidized housing.

Use exact-date, feature-adjusted local Mietspiegel / comparable rents, not a city-wide portal mean. Separately test parking and legitimately allocated service charges. Compute €/m² using correct living area, not room area. For WG contracts, aggregate per-room rent and shared-space allocation, check full dwelling/individual contract law and whether the arrangement is lawful. § 549 exceptions for genuine temporary use or a furnished room **within the landlord's own occupied dwelling** are narrow; ordinary third-party furnished WG lets are not automatically exempt from Mietpreisbremse. Document each furnishing surcharge and basis, if relevant. If legal evaluation is ambiguous or disputed, put the legal rent and decision on HOLD for qualified review.

## AfA and lawful rental tax effect

- Verify EStG §§ 7 and 21 (rental income), § 6(1) no.1a (three-year/15% acquisition-related building modernization classification), and §23 (private-property sale timing); consider specific regime and grandfathering. Typical straight-line residential building depreciation under §7(4) depends on completion year (2.5% for eligible pre-1925, 2% for 1925–2022, 3% for post-2022). A higher rate such as 4.3% or 7% is **not standard** just because a seller quotes it: require defensible shortened remaining useful life or a valid special statutory method, proof and professional review. Do not apply both degressive and straight-line deductions on the same basis.
- Allocate purchase price and attributable purchase costs between **building and non-depreciable land** under a defensible market-value method. Cross-check seller's 76–95% "building share" with the BMF Kaufpreisaufteilung work aid, independent property data and evidence. Flag furniture/parking as potentially separate tax assets and accounting. Account for purchase incentives or sales commissions with their legally correct basis/treatment, rather than presuming a deduction or reimbursement.
- Separate rental receipts, interest, deductible operating expenses, **actual** maintenance spending and AfA from financing **principal** (not a rental tax deduction). Transfers into WEG repair reserves are not automatically currently deductible merely when paid; research timing and use.
- Compare total household income tax **with vs without the candidate**, preserving existing Mannheim rental loss, loss limitations, ownership shares, spouse income, allowances, social contributions and actual forecast tax capacity. Tax-class withholding changes are not the same as annual assessed liability. A flat "25.6% return on a loss" is only a labeled rough sensitivity unless justified by an actual incremental tax calculation. Reconcile any projected monthly payroll adjustment against Jahressteuer and future repayments.
- Test whether sustained large deductions and poor economic yield imply a missing commercial profit intention / Liebhaberei issue; seek expert opinion where material. If job income disappears, assume **zero usable short-term tax rebate** until proven otherwise.
- Model capital gains and tax on any sale inside the §23 window; for exits outside it, verify precise acquisition/disposal timing, personal vs corporate ownership and exceptions, and do not assert blanket tax exemption.

**Sources**: [EStG §7](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/estg/__7.html), [§21](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/estg/__21.html), [§23](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/estg/__23.html), [§6](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/estg/__6.html), [BMF purchase allocation (2026)](https://www.bundesfinanzministerium.de/Content/DE/Downloads/Steuern/Berechnung-Aufteilung-Grundstueckskaufpreis.html), [WEG §28](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/woeigg/__28.html). Read current BMF guidance and, for material issues, BFH decisions.

## What "Mietgarantie" can actually mean

Classify the instrument: (a) seller's contractual payment promise; (b) rent guarantee from professional operator (possibly with sublet/WG strategy); (c) a Mietausfallversicherung with named insured perils, waiting times, deductibles and exclusions; (d) a public/private lease or prepaid rent. These are **not equivalent**.

Demand signed contract, provider legal entity, audited financial standing where obtainable, payment trigger, coverage cap, duration, security/collateral, vacancy/default exclusions, rent-regulation compliance, assignability on sale, rights on insolvency, claim process and cancellation. Check ultimate occupant rent against law even when the operator promises a higher master rent. Never classify a rent guarantee as cash-equivalent, backed by a bank or insurer, or renewable automatically without evidence.

Underwrite **unsubsidized, legally collectible market rent from the day the guarantee expires**. Present an additional provider-default scenario and do not use seller payments to artificially raise permanent ROI.

## Other diligence and lawful use

Check WEG community documents and funded reserve, Sonderumlagen, building condition, energy certificate and renovations; occupancy/vacancy and locks on owner occupation; regional short-term, furnished and room-by-room use restrictions. Inspections, lawyer/notary advice, lender approval, and Steuerberater input are necessary where legal/tax ambiguity affects decision.
