# Annual law, deadlines, and calculations

## Establish an annual rule register

Before giving material advice or calculating final amounts, browse official sources for the selected assessment year. Store each rule's key, value/method, tax year, effective/application dates, authority/section, directly supporting URL, publication/version date, checked date, and unresolved conditions. Constants in examples and prior returns are never defaults.

Source order: applicable enacted law and transitional provisions; applicable treaty/protocol/MLI and official publication; year-specific BMF guidance and official handbooks; year's ELSTER instructions and official state operational guidance; relevant official BFH/BVerfG judgments. Distinguish statutory rule, administration interpretation, binding judgment, pending dispute, and agent inference. A blog or community skill can suggest a research lead, never establish a tax rule.

Read the linked page itself and verify title, year, jurisdiction, taxpayer category, scope and supporting passage. A nearby search result, different-year form, or Feststellung instruction cannot substantiate a personal KAP field. Cite the specific rule beside each material conclusion. If sources conflict, check effective dates and rank, explain the unresolved point, and do not quietly choose the larger refund.

Current consolidated statutes may include later amendments. For historical returns, use annual EStH/LStH, official promulgated amendments/application rules, and archived year-specific form instructions. Check enacted changes since the return year, including provisions applicable to open cases. Do not treat proposals or press speculation as law.

## Parameters to verify when relevant

| Area | Annual verification targets |
|---|---|
| Filing | Mandatory/voluntary basis, ordinary/advised deadlines, individual demands, valid extensions, weekend/state-holiday adjustment, limitation periods |
| Tax computation | §32a tariff and splitting, basic allowance, solidarity surcharge exemption/taper, state church tax and capital-tax interaction |
| Employment | Employee lump sum, commute distance bands/rates, public transport comparison, daily remote-work/office rules, equipment depreciation, travel/meal/relocation schedules |
| Household | Child allowances and benefits, eligibility/months/country reductions, childcare share/cap, support limits, training and single-parent relief |
| Personal expenses | Pension/insurance limits, medical reasonable burden, disability/care allowances, donation limits/carryforwards |
| Property | AfA regimes and qualifying dates, special deductions, subsidized/energy schemes, rental discount tests, capitalization and repair spreading rules |
| Investments | Sparer-Pauschbetrag, loss categories and transition law, fund classification/partial exemptions, annual basis interest, deemed receipt date, historical lots |
| International | Treaty applicability and method by income, employment allocation guidance, FX conventions, foreign tax relief and reclaim limits |
| Business | VAT/small-business and trade-tax rules, investment/depreciation rules, separate returns and electronic filing obligations |

A calendar year return can contain amounts economically calculated for another year. In particular, reconcile each fund's Vorabpauschale calculation year, statutory deemed-receipt date, and assessment year; do not attach it to the year in its title automatically.

## Deadline triage

Verify obligation using facts and §§25/46 EStG, §149 AO and applicable transitional rules; voluntary employee assessments have different limitation rules. A Finanzamt demand can create a concrete deadline. Adviser extensions require actual qualifying representation, not this skill. Check late-filing surcharge, interest, extensions and objections separately. If overdue, prioritize a complete truthful filing and appropriate extension/contact draft while continuing preparation; never fabricate a timely date or silently omit income.

For orientation only: official ELSTER 2025 instructions state 31 July 2026 as the ordinary mandatory deadline and 31 December 2029 for the voluntary assessment application. Reverify case applicability and current notices at use time. These dates do not generalize to earlier pandemic extensions, advised cases, agriculture, or subsequent years. [ELSTER 2025](https://www.elster.de/eportal/helpGlobal?themaGlobal=help_est_ufa_10_2025).

## Calculate reproducibly

Use exact decimal EUR arithmetic, recorded FX inputs, and rule-specific rounding. Separate source income and expenses, exempt/progression amounts, reliefs, taxable income, tariff/separate capital tax, tax reductions, Soli/church tax, and credits/prepayments. Do not add the employee lump sum on top of actual employment expenses. Do not turn a deduction into a euro-for-euro refund.

Prefer the complete ELSTER preliminary calculation or a documented year-compatible tax engine over a hand-built approximation for splitting, progression, capital elections, Soli/church tax, extraordinary income, or foreign tax caps. A BMF wage-tax calculator/PAP models payroll withholding; it is not a complete personal annual income-tax computation. An independent worksheet checks reconciliations but does not replace missing engine capabilities. Label an unsupported component uncomputed rather than reporting an exact household refund.

Compare options with the same facts, tax year, withholding and prepayments. Where available evaluate joint vs two separate assessments, capital Günstigerprüfung/withholding review, lawful expense allocations, repair-cost spreading and loss carryback/carryforward elections, foreign credit/deduction elections, and competing energy/household subsidies. Check eligibility before comparison. Show total liability and multi-year consequences, not only today's cash refund. The largest refund may merely reflect larger prepayments.
