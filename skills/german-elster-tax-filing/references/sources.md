# Official source directory and tool provenance

Research starting points, not a frozen statement of law. Initial research: 2026-10-27. Open the applicable page at use time; record its year/effective version and the passage supporting the actual claim. Annual URLs below demonstrate 2025 pages; locate and verify other years rather than assuming a URL pattern resolves correctly. Source unavailability means unverified, not permission to answer from memory.

Some BMF/handbook endpoints intermittently return 503 or redirect during automated retrieval. Search the official domain for the document title/reference and use another directly supporting official publication, annual handbook or ELSTER instruction where it covers the issue. A failed fetch alone does not prove the URL is obsolete. Do not treat search snippets or a different-scope page as substitutes for reading the authority; keep the unresolved treatment visible when no authoritative text is retrievable.
