# Rental property and co-ownership

## Build a property register

For every object/unit record country/address/reference, economic owner and shares by period, acquisition/inheritance/gift date, purchase contract and benefits/burdens transfer date, completion year, actual use/area, letting availability, related tenants, and disposal. Distinguish long-term residential, commercial, short-term/holiday, subletting, bare land, leasehold and investment partnership interests. Do not collapse multiple properties into one net line.

Collect deeds/settlement, land/building valuation, acquisition costs, prior AfA and donor/predecessor schedules, leases, deposits, monthly rent/operating-cost receipts, refunds/arrears, manager/WEG accounts, loan statements, insurance/tax/repair invoices, grants and vacancy marketing evidence. Reconcile gross amounts and platform fees separately; a net platform payout alone is incomplete. Distinguish refundable deposits from income according to actual use and legal treatment.

## Ownership and separate determinations

For spouses' co-owned property verify whether income can be dealt with in their joint assessment or requires separate determination. For sibling, inheritance community, GbR or other co-owners check §§179/180 AO and exceptions; a separate gesonderte und einheitliche Feststellung can be required. Prepare property totals, participant shares, special expenses, and reconcile the allocated amount to the personal return. Do not allocate everything to the payer or import the same property profit both directly and as a participation. A Feststellungsbescheid is a separate underlying assessment with its own remedy route.

Year-specific routing examples: individual property schedules generally use Anlage V; short-term/holiday letting may additionally require V-FeWo; participation income can use V-Sonstige. Feststellung forms differ: official 2025 instructions include FE-V and FE-V-Objekt. Verify current-year labels and source scope instead of assuming older FE-V nomenclature still applies. [ELSTER personal 2025](https://www.elster.de/eportal/helpGlobal?themaGlobal=help_est_ufa_10_2025), [ELSTER Feststellung 2025](https://www.elster.de/eportal/helpGlobal?themaGlobal=help_fein_2025).

## Reconcile income and expenses

Maintain per-object gross rent, operating-cost advances/settlements, other receipts, eligible financing interest, maintenance, management, insurance, taxes and AfA. Apply payment/accrual and recurring-payment exceptions correctly for the income type. Identify VAT treatment where relevant; do not casually move between gross and net amounts.

Loan principal repayments are not current rental deductions. Trace interest and refinancing to actual use of borrowed funds, especially mixed private/rental loans. Land is not depreciable. Split purchase price and ancillary costs between land/building and any separate assets using supported valuation, not a refund-driven arbitrary ratio. Verify linear/degressive/special AfA eligibility, statutory dates, start month, useful-life evidence and inherited/gifted basis. Separate private/rental area and periods; track any change of use. [§7 EStG](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/estg/__7.html), [§9 EStG](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/estg/__9.html).

## Repairs versus capital expenditure

Create an invoice-level work schedule with scope, completion/payment dates, VAT, net amount and property/building. Test normal maintenance, extensions/improvements, acquisition-related construction, and separate assets. Under §6(1) no.1a EStG screen qualifying works within three years after acquisition against the 15% building-acquisition-cost threshold excluding VAT in the numerator; determine scope/exceptions before calculating. Threshold failure can change treatment of the whole qualifying pool, not just the excess. Reconcile prior-year works and later invoices; do not evaluate each tax year in isolation. Gifts/inheritances and mixed ownership require separate basis analysis. [§6 EStG](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/estg/__6.html).

Keep threshold arithmetic separate from depreciation basis: qualifying works enter the threshold test net of VAT, while capitalized cost can include nonrecoverable VAT. Establish input-tax recoverability rather than adding the net threshold pool directly to building basis. If later qualifying works cross the acquisition-window threshold, review and recompute earlier years' immediate deductions and AfA; identify necessary assessment changes and the applicable §175 AO procedure, including already assessed years. Preserve originals and prepare the amendment for scoped approval. [§9b EStG](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/estg/__9b.html), [§175 AO](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/ao_1977/__175.html), [EStH 2025 acquisition-related expenditure guidance](https://usth.bundesfinanzministerium.de/esth/2025/B-Anhaenge/Anhang-30/V-neu/anhang-30-V-neu.html).

WEG reserve contributions are generally not themselves the currently deductible repair expense; reconcile actual spending, its nature, allocation and manager evidence. For eligible residential maintenance consider §82b EStDV spreading versus immediate deduction, effects on other years, transfers and death; the eligibility and election must be verified. Capital costs recover through the appropriate depreciation regime rather than being relabelled repairs. [§82b EStDV](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/estdv_1955/__82b.html).

## Letting conditions and disposals

For discounted/related-party letting compare contractual rent to applicable local market rent including the required operating-cost basis, actual payment and arm's-length conduct. Verify the year's thresholds and any total-surplus forecast requirement; don't apply a single percentage to every year. Document vacancy letting intent and rental/private use of holiday homes. Short-term services can change business/VAT classification. Check platforms' reports against complete income; reporting thresholds do not create income-tax exemptions. [§21 EStG](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/estg/__21.html).

For sales establish binding contract dates, history and exemptions, inherited/gifted predecessor dates, selling costs and depreciation consequences; investigate private disposal and commercial property-trading classification. Do not assume ten years' ownership excludes every business or special-rule issue. [§23 EStG](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/estg/__23.html).

Foreign property also requires the cross-border module: compute under German rules, research treaty relief/progression and country-specific limits. No universal rule exempts all foreign rent. Escalate disputed basis, useful life, commercial activity, abuse, complex communities or material treaty uncertainty while preparing the supported schedules.
