# Other income and scope boundaries

## Self-employment, trades, and agriculture

Screen freelance/consulting, online content, platform sales, side businesses, partnerships, commercial letting/services, PV installations and farming. Classify activity and owner before selecting S/G/L, EÜR/balance sheet or determination returns. Gross cash receipts are not automatically profit. Collect books, invoices, expenses, inventory/assets, depreciation, private-use contributions/withdrawals, grants, losses, tax prepayments and registrations.

Check accounting method, accrual/cash and recurring-payment rules, deductible versus private expenses, investment deductions, VAT inclusion and loss restrictions. Do not replace bookkeeping with a chat estimate. Income-tax classification, VAT/small-business treatment and trade-tax duties are separate; thresholds/eligibility change by year. A personal-income-tax return does not complete UStVA, annual VAT, Gewerbesteuer, payroll, separate entity or partnership obligations. Identify each return, owner, period and deadline and work on it only within the user's scope. An optional ELSTER tool for VAT cannot transmit an income-tax return.

For PV, verify income-tax exemption separately from VAT zero-rating, purchase/commissioning/extension date, unit/building/person limits and any continuing business implications. Do not reuse earlier size/turnover thresholds without checking amendments. Partnerships can require participant allocations, special business expenses and separate determinations; use the actual year's forms.

## Retirement, pensions, and maintenance

Collect statutory/private/occupational/foreign pension notices, commencement year, prior tax-free component, annual adjustments, contributions and withholding. Determine taxable share or earnings component for the actual pension type; do not apply a single retirement percentage universally. Select R, R-AV/bAV, R-AUS or other forms only after classification and treaty review. Handle received maintenance, annuities and other recurring benefits under the applicable regime; do not confuse payer's deduction with recipient's tax treatment.

## Exceptional events

Screen gifts/inheritances, estate communities, divorce/death, property disposals, sale of business or company interest, compensation, cancellation of debt, disasters and litigation. Gift/inheritance tax and notification obligations can exist separately from income tax; receipt is not automatically income, but later yield or inherited tax basis can affect this return. Obtain wills/transfer terms, predecessor schedules and assessments when relevant.

Research special circumstances from actual facts rather than forcing them into an ordinary form: agriculture, shipping, cross-border pensions, international organizations, stock deferrals, foreign entities, carried interests, investment partnerships and complex trusts. Use official law/guidance, record unresolved questions and assemble a professional handoff.

## Review threshold and unlisted cases

No checklist covers all German tax situations. When an unlisted fact appears, identify the economic event, owner, country, dates, legal category, evidence and possible separate duties; add it to the case coverage matrix, then research it. Do not silently ignore it because the skill lacks a dedicated row.

Recommend licensed review for material unresolved legal classification, disputed residency/treaty/foreign structure, difficult valuation or ownership, suspected evasion, deliberate prior omissions, aggressive arrangements, insolvency, or a tax-office investigation. Continue authorized document collection, arithmetic, supported schedules and deadline tracking. State precisely which entry/election requires review and why. Do not imply that use of AI creates professional privilege, representation or Steuerberater status. [§2 StBerG](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stberg/__2.html).

If prior filed information appears wrong, preserve originals, establish amounts/years and limitation/deadline facts, research correction obligations under §153 AO and obtain professional help for potential tax offences. Do not describe an ordinary amendment as a guaranteed valid self-disclosure or send one without scoped approval. [§153 AO](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/ao_1977/__153.html).
